The class

Beat-based, and actually fun

Everything runs to remixes and music people already know, which is what keeps it feeling like something you'd want to do again rather than a workout you have to get through. It's full body, so arms, glutes, legs, and core, and I use Bala Bangles and bands instead of heavy equipment. Very little gear, a genuinely good workout.

I offer modifications for everything and I meet you where you're at. I've taught long enough to read a room, so I adjust as we go and make sure everyone in the group is comfortable. You should leave excited and ready to take on the rest of your day.

Classes run 30 or 45 minutes, whichever fits your schedule better.
